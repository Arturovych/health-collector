Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Jarvis Health Collector ("the app") is a personal tool used by a single person, its developer, to download their own Fitbit data from the Google Health API. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to that data.

Data the app accesses

With the consent given on the Google sign-in screen, the app reads the following Google Health data of the signed-in account, in read-only mode: activity and fitness, sleep, health metrics and measurements, electrocardiogram and irregular rhythm notifications, nutrition, exercise location, profile and settings.

How the data is used

The data is used for one purpose: to keep a local copy of the account owner's own health records and to show them to that same person as charts and summaries.

Storage

All data is stored only on the account owner's own computer. The app has no server and no cloud storage. The Google sign-in tokens are also stored only on that computer.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not shared and not transferred to any third party. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any generalised machine-learning or AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The local copy is kept until the account owner deletes it from their computer. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after that the app can no longer read any data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue in the app's GitHub repository.
