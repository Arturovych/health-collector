Jarvis Health Collector

Jarvis Health Collector is a personal, non-commercial tool. It downloads its owner's own Fitbit data from the Google Health API and stores it on the owner's own computer, so that the owner can look at their sleep, heart rate and activity history in one place.

What it does

  • Signs in with Google and asks for read-only access to Google Health data.
  • Downloads the signed-in person's own records: sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, steps, distance, calories, exercise sessions and related measurements recorded by a Fitbit device.
  • Saves these records in a local archive on the same computer.

What it does not do

  • It does not change or delete anything in Google Health: access is read-only.
  • It does not send the data to any server, service or other person.
  • It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
